# The Horners' Big Trip

A simple one-page tracker so family and friends can follow our trip through Europe and Bali (29 Nov 2026 – 11 Jan 2027).

It shows:
- **Where we are now**: the city, the local time there and what's on today (worked out from the date, no tracking).
- **Our route** on a map of Europe, plus the whole trip.
- **Each stop** with its dates and highlights. Past stops fade and tick off as we go.

What it deliberately doesn't include: booking references, accommodation names or addresses, prices, phone numbers or travel documents.

## Viewing it

Open the GitHub Pages link for this repository. To preview a particular date, add `?now=2026-12-24T12:00` to the end of the address.

## Updating it

Everything is in `index.html`. The trip data sits in the `DATA` block near the bottom of the file, so to change it, replace `index.html` with a newly generated copy and commit.
